## 🎯 Purpose & Philosophy

**Gym for Communication** is a personal training environment for communication and professional character. It bridges the gap between thought and articulation through daily structured exercises, repetition, and transparent rubric-based feedback.

> *"There is no race. There is only rhythm."*  
> *"Structure is not a cage; it is the rhythm that turns effort into expression."*

---

## 🏗️ Architecture & 21-Layer Compliance

1. **Layer 3 — Welcoming Onboarding**:
   - 4-step sequence: Welcome & Daily Rhythm $\to$ Multi-goal Selection $\to$ Preferences (Session length, confidence rating 1-10) $\to$ Progressive Roadmap (30/60/90 days).
2. **Layer 4 — Responsive Application Shell**:
   - Compact left sidebar for desktop and accessible touch bottom-nav for mobile.
   - Dynamic Dashboard adapting to new vs. returning practitioners.
3. **Layer 5 — 30-Day Starter Curriculum (Topic Ladder)**:
   - **Stage A (Days 1–10)**: Foundation & Self-Expression (Comfort).
   - **Stage B (Days 11–20)**: Observation & Interpersonal Communication (Challenge).
   - **Stage C (Days 21–30)**: Professional Expression & Influence (Command).
4. **Layer 6 — G.A.I.N. & Structural Guideline Frameworks**:
   - **G**reet / Frame $\to$ **A**cknowledge Context $\to$ **I**nsight $\to$ **N**avigate Out.
   - Illustrative examples and common mistakes to avoid for every exercise.
5. **Layer 7 — Practice Workspace**:
   - Distraction-free response editor with real-time character/word count.
   - Browser Web Speech API integration with transparent limitation disclosures.
   - Local draft auto-saving.
6. **Layer 8 — AI Communication Expert & 4-Dimension Rubric**:
   - Evaluates across: **Clarity & Brevity**, **Structure & Flow**, **Specificity & Evidence**, **Language Effectiveness**.
   - Identifies strongest element, main priority, and concrete evidence from user text.
7. **Layer 9 — Raw Baseline Attempt & Revision Integrity**:
   - Preserves original baseline attempts separately from revisions.
   - Side-by-side comparison with score differences.
8. **Layer 10 — Direct / Harsh Critique Mode**:
   - User-controlled toggle for unvarnished, demanding critique without personal degradation.
9. **Layer 11 — Targeted Micro Drills**:
   - Weakness-focused drill assignments with target skill, instructions, and completion criteria.
10. **Layer 12 — Observable Growth Analytics**:
    - Derived strictly from saved practice sessions (no fabricated vanity stats).
    - Compassionate consistency tracker (no shaming or resetting on rest days).
11. **Layer 13 — Reflection Vault ("Document → Defend")**:
    - Chronological timeline with search, filtering, and JSON/Markdown export.
12. **Layer 14 — Settings & Personalization**:
    - Configurable product name, goals, session length, and AI providers (Local Heuristics / Gemini API / OpenAI API).
13. **Layer 16 — Provider-Independent AI Boundary**:
    - Client-side heuristic analyzer for zero-dependency offline practice, plus live Gemini/OpenAI API configuration.
