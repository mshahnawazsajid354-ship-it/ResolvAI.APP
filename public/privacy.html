Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

ResolvAI is a communication practice app. It is currently a working prototype built by M.S. Sajid ("we", "us"). This page explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

1. What we collect

  • If you sign up with Google: your email address, your name and your Google account ID, which Google provides when you approve sign-in. We never receive your Google password. We request only basic profile and email access, and we do not read your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.
  • Your practice: the answers you write or dictate, your scores, reflections and settings. These are saved in your browser, on your own device.
  • Basic technical data, such as your IP address, which our hosting and security systems process to run the service and prevent abuse.

2. How we use it

  • To create and manage your account, give you AI feedback on your practice, keep the service secure and within usage limits, and contact you about your account or this policy.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising. We send marketing emails only if you clearly opt in. That option is off by default.

3. AI feedback

When you submit a practice for feedback, the text of your answer and the practice details needed to generate feedback are sent to a third-party AI service provider, and the feedback is returned to you. Please do not put sensitive personal information, such as passwords, health or financial details, into your answers. AI responses can have mistakes.

4. Voice dictation

If you use the microphone button, speech recognition is provided by your browser. In some browsers, such as Chrome, your browser may send your audio to its provider to turn speech into text. We do not receive or store your audio.

5. Who handles your data

  • Google, to sign you in.
  • Supabase, which stores account details (name and email) in a database hosted in Seoul, South Korea.
  • Vercel, which hosts the app.
  • The third-party AI service provider described above.

These providers process data only to provide their service to us.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account details while your account exists. To delete your account, email us at the address below and we will delete it within 30 days. Practice data stored on your device can be removed any time by clearing your browser's site data.

7. Your choices

You can use ResolvAI without an account by choosing "Skip for now". You can ask us to access, correct or delete your account information by email.

8. Children

ResolvAI is not intended for children under 16.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No online service is completely secure, and ResolvAI is still a prototype.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
